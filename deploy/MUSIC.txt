RIFT VALLEY MUSIC | 0.13.3

FOUR FINISHED LOOPS
Lobby.wav: Overlook Afterglow, 78 BPM. Warm pads, soft bells and a relaxed bass line.
CrystalGrove.wav: Glassfang Pursuit, 150 BPM. Bright crystal arpeggios and driving drums.
SunsetRuins.wav: Ruin Runners, 160 BPM. Low percussion and urgent modal synth figures.
NeonHarbor.wav: Harbor Overdrive, 172 BPM. Fast electronic bass and synth lead.
All four tracks live in assets/music. They are original synthesized compositions,
not third-party recordings. Stereo 44.1 kHz PCM WAV, below 20 MB each, with loop seams.

CONNECTED GAME MUSIC
The game now references four public APMOfficial Creator Store releases:
Lobby: Relaxed Scene (1848354536), a gentle melodic theme.
Crystal Grove: Intense Chase (1848090337), action pursuit music.
Sunset Ruins: Chaser (9042664292), dark percussion and driving tribal rhythms.
Neon Harbor: Classic Chase (1837560230), electronic club pursuit music.
The official listings and credits are in assets/music/credits.json.
These are streamed Roblox assets. No custom upload is needed for these selections.
The 0.13.3 Studio probes loaded all four tracks and reported IsPlaying with
positive duration. These probes use zero volume; audible review and privately
published playback remain separate checks.
No audio recordings have been downloaded or included in the release archive.

OPTIONAL: SWITCH TO THE ORIGINAL COMPOSITIONS
1. Open the current Rift Valley build in Studio and publish your own private experience.
2. Open Asset Manager, choose Import, and select the four WAV files from assets/music.
3. Wait for each import and its audio moderation/processing to complete.
4. Copy each audio asset ID. Grant the Rift Valley experience usage permission for
   every private audio asset in Creator Dashboard if Roblox requires it.
5. From this project folder in PowerShell, run:
   .\build\Configure-Audio.ps1
   Enter LobbyAudioId, CrystalGroveAudioId, SunsetRuinsAudioId and NeonHarborAudioId
   when prompted. The script connects IDs, validates/rebuilds the place and packages it.
6. Stop the Studio test, close the old tab and reopen dist/RiftValley-0.13.0.rbxl.
   Test both lobby and each map. Republish the updated place to the same experience.

PLAYBACK
Spectators hear the chill lobby loop. Ready/Crossing participants hear their map's
intense score. Tracks crossfade over 1.5 seconds and loop. The music button mutes
locally; muting never changes another player's volume. Missing/unloaded IDs stay silent.
