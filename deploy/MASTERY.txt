RIFT MASTERY | 0.14.0

ACCESS
Lobby menu > Mastery, or the garden gateway behind the fountain.
The gateway now has a stationary glowing 3D trophy and a small overhead name.
The menu uses six native 3D trophy/medal viewports with earned medal colors.
Choose a theme and a reached course. Reaching a later course in a chase unlocks
practice permanently. Existing profiles migrate prior course progress.
Practice uses isolated copies with the selected theme's geometry and hazards.
It has no chasers. Retry, a fall or movement violation resets just that attempt.
A successful practice finish stores a practice best; it grants no race rewards.
The trainee can retry, change course, or return to the lobby.

RACE MEDALS
Each of the 15 theme/course pairs stores one best race time and highest medal.
A legitimate finish earns bronze. Meet silver/gold targets for higher medals.
Medal points use bronze=1, silver=2, gold=3; maximum 45 points. Repeating a
course improves its record rather than accumulating duplicate medal progress.
All times and rewards come from the server; clients cannot submit completions.
Intermediate course finishes save medals and records without popup cards.
Practice completion and the fifth race finish show a brief two-line medal/time
badge. View detailed records, medal totals and targets in the Mastery menu.
Read Config.Mastery for the editable initial gold/silver times and theme scales.
Gold seconds by course: 12/15/17/18/20. Silver: 20/25/29/33/35.
Grove scale is 1, Ruins 1.05, Harbor 1.10.
These targets require human playtesting and tuning before public release.

EARNED REWARDS
Grove shimmer trail: medals on 3 Crystal Grove courses.
Sunset ribbon trail: medals on 3 Sunset Ruins courses.
Neon current trail: medals on 3 Neon Harbor courses.
Grove Guardian skin: medals on all 5 Crystal Grove courses.
Ruins Sentinel skin: medals on all 5 Sunset Ruins courses.
Harbor Phantom skin: medals on all 5 Neon Harbor courses.
Crystal Bloom victory: 6 medal points.
Sunset Sparks victory: silver or gold on 5 distinct theme/course pairs.
Neon Fireworks victory: gold on 5 distinct theme/course pairs.

Equip rewards in Shop > Trails or Style Unlocks. Skin and Victory slots work
independently from Aura, Title and Trail. Availability derives from saved race
medals, rather than stored ownership flags. Mastery skins recolor existing
geometry and preserve the skeleton, mass, collision and warning-eye colors.
Victory effects stay local within 140 studs, last about 1.3 seconds, and share
a 90-particle cap across simultaneous celebrations.

PERSISTENCE AND LIMITS
No DataStore reset. Old profiles keep currency, purchased trails and cosmetic
preferences. Layout revision 2 archives previous race/practice best times as
legacyBestMs and starts fresh bestMs for the rebuilt courses. Existing medals,
unlocked courses and earned styles remain. New records preserve highest medals.
Live saves include these records and cosmetic preferences. Unpublished Studio
remains session-only.
Geometry/hazards share one copy for equal course/theme requests, up to 15 copies
across the whole server. The final trainee leaving releases that copy. Practice
has independent clocks, movement limits and fall recovery; main races continue.
Studio testing must verify actual movement, replication, layered cameras and
mobile frame rates. Offline suites do not reproduce these engine behaviors.
