RIFT VALLEY 0.15.0 | PLAYTEST SESSION

Use 5-10 new players across computer and touch devices. Watch their first run
without explaining the controls. These are proposed test goals, not measured results.

1. FIRST MINUTE
Can the player find the route, learn sprint/dash from the short prompts, and
reach the first finish without help? Note the first moment of confusion.
Every next-match lobby lasts 30 seconds; Ready lasts 3 seconds, including solo.
Run Again must not skip or restart the lobby countdown.

2. FAIR CONTACT
Ask players to try standing still, walking away, sprinting, dashing sideways
and jumping. They should see/hear the warning before capture. A missed grab
gives the monster a recovery pause and the runner brief protection.
Bruisers have amber eyes and pursue at 29/30/31/32/32.5 by course. Cyan stalkers
pursue at 32.25/33/33.5 in courses 3-5, below sprint speed 34. They have cyan eyes,
a planted warning and a committed straight charge. Introduce them in course 3.
Test two monsters reaching one runner so attacks never overlap against that runner.

3. ROUTE CHOICES
Walk the alternating mint-edged crossings through Broken Causeway and Rift Gauntlet.
Try the shorter central route and its gold bonus crystals. Inspect every junction.
Grove bridges grant a short speed boost. Ruins stones flash before disappearing
and restore after three seconds. Harbor cargo moves sideways; wide passages remain.
Check these interactions with default jumps and touch controls in Studio physics.

4. REPLAY AND FIRST REWARD
Lose a solo run: the next run follows the full 30-second lobby and Ready.
Run Again can skip results but cannot bypass the queue. Lobby pauses enrollment;
Play rejoins the next run when its countdown ends.
Finish three legitimate courses, including solo, to unlock Rift Scout. If the
title slot was empty, it equips automatically. An existing title stays selected.
Solo earns half base coins/XP and course progress; it never grants competitive
wins/tags or paid Double Coins multipliers. Live profile persistence needs Roblox.
Finish all five: return to the supported lobby and use parkour while others finish.

5. COLLECT EVIDENCE
Open View > Output in Studio and filter for [RiftPlaytest] in the server output.
Reports appear after each round and at shutdown. They aggregate starts, course
finishes, escapes, retries, dodges, falls/catches by course and departure course.
They also show average first-course completion time since joining.
Every 30 seconds the client submits an averaged frame-rate sample. The server
validates/rate limits samples and separates touch and desktop averages. These
are client-reported observations, not authoritative performance measurements.
Reports contain no player names or user IDs. They remain in server Output.
Manually record unclear captures and device issues in the supplied CSV.

6. TUNE FROM OBSERVATION
Prioritize unexplained deaths, unsupported ground, obstructed cameras and missed
touch inputs. Then adjust stamina, monster speed and obstacle spacing one at a
time. Compare the next group against the previous group before adding difficulty.
Test 2, 4 and eventually 30 players. Offline checks do not reproduce Roblox
physics, real NPC pathfinding, network latency or mobile device performance.

7. BONUS-COURSE REGRESSION
Enter courses 2-5 on all three themes. Catchers should head toward you immediately
during the 2.5-second safe arrival, without attacking until protection expires.
They should keep pressure while running, advance past floor waypoints, jump
hurdles once and take supported routes around gaps and crumbling shortcuts.
Dodge charges toward gaps and moving cargo; catchers must stop before losing
support, then resume pursuit. Force a fall and observe stable recovery without
a lingering charge or immediate grab. Test split multiplayer groups too.
Mint battery icons restore stamina only; cyan wind icons reset dash only.
Both should show glowing solid 3D artwork with no labels or BillboardGui.
Collect each while its resource is depleted; verify separate seven-second
cooldowns and local dimming, then restoration when the resource is ready.

8. RIFT MASTERY AND PRACTICE
Open Mastery from the menu and the garden gateway. Verify locked course buttons
until that course has been reached in a real run. Check all three themes and
phone portrait/landscape. Browsing should pause solo replay; Play resumes it.
In multiplayer, let one player practice while others race. Main round changes
must not move the trainee or reset their clock/hazards. Main catchers cannot
capture trainees. Practice has no chasers and awards no race rewards or medals.
Falls and Retry should restore entry and full movement resources. Finish once:
check the separate practice best, frozen timer and single result card. Leave:
return to the lobby; when the final trainee leaves, remove that course copy.
Run every selected theme/course in Studio to verify physical support and camera
visibility in the isolated layers. Check the connected map score and crossfade.

9. MEDALS AND EARNED STYLES
Time race courses from movement unlock, excluding Ready. Compare recorded time
with a stopwatch. Finish at/slower/faster than silver and gold targets. Replay:
best times and highest medals must never decrease or award duplicate points.
Try to forge a finish via a remote or teleport; no medal should result.
Complete three/five distinct courses in a theme to unlock its trail/skin.
Earn 6 points, 5 silver-or-gold medals, and 5 gold medals for the victory styles.
Equip each skin and compare reach, warning-eye color and movement with default.
Win with each victory effect, including simultaneous winners on mobile. Check
that effects remain local, brief, noncolliding and capped. Test real save/rejoin
after private publishing; Studio intentionally retains session-only progress.
Use observed beginner and skilled-player times to tune provisional gold/silver
targets. Record median times and failure causes before changing difficulty.

10. COURSE VARIETY, DETAIL AND PRESSURE
Run all 15 course/theme pairs. Compare Shattered Steps' hurdles/raised shortcut,
Serpent Slalom's split routes, Broken Causeway's alternating crossings and Rift
Gauntlet's combined weave/jump sections. Hugging one edge must not bypass them.
Walk the supported detours, then try the quicker jumping route. Confirm normal
avatar jumps reach each shortcut and that missed later-course jumps can fall.
Inspect trees, fungi, columns, banners, cranes, cables and surface detail from
player height. Decorative geometry must leave lanes, buffs and cameras readable.
Watch Harbor cargo at both extremes. Test multiple catchers following a turning
player; offline route checks do not prove engine navmesh or jumping behavior.
Try walking without abilities, waste stamina/dash, then use them deliberately.
Catchers should punish slow or wasteful play while allowing skilled escape.
Check catchers start ahead, face entry and pursue immediately during protected
arrivals. Ready prepares paths without moving them. They cannot grab until 2.5
seconds after Ready ends. Catch warnings,
cover and successful dodge protection must still work.
Buff models must gently bob without rotating, keep their collection anchor fixed,
and show native neon/glow even without resource GUI. Compare two players: one
on cooldown sees dim models while the other's available buffs stay bright.
Practice the same layouts and verify real movement support in every theme.
Load a revision-1 mastery fixture: retain medals/unlocks/cosmetics, archive old
race/practice PBs, then record fresh times on revision 2 without duplicate medals.
Record human times before tuning the revised provisional medal targets.

11. EXTENDED LOBBY PARKOUR AND QUEUE
Verify the spawn has no confetti icon. Completing parkour and victory effects
may still celebrate automatically. Start Rift Ascent and land on all 24 stones
in order. Inspect its narrow beams, small pads, upper loop and open finish, with no return icon.
Fall after stones 5, 11, 17 and 23: the attempt ends. Retry returns to the
entry plate and starts a new timer on landing. Do not award completion for stepping on a later stone early.
The existing ceiling must clear the final running jump and camera. Check a race
starting mid-lap leaves the active parkour session and timer uninterrupted.
Count the 30-second lobby before the first and following matches, including solo.
Press Run Again repeatedly: the queue must keep its original deadline. Verify
map voting remains available while waiting and Pause/Play preserve earned items.
Version 0.12.0 automated Studio tests covered engine traversal and individual
jumps. Repeat with the current test copies before treating this release as engine
verified. Add human/mobile observations for landings, camera views and difficulty.

12. PHYSICAL PLATES AND AMBIENCE
Step on all three map plates while grounded. Confirm one shared vote per player,
vote changes when entering another plate, and the Maps menu has no vote buttons.
The Next Map sidebar must show the same three shared totals for every waiting
player and mark each player's own selection. Vote changes/departures update it.
Check portrait, small-phone and landscape layouts; open menus and race start
hide it. Inspect the floating crystal/temple/anchor emblems from all sides:
small map names remain visible without a board or pole. The selected emblem
brightens locally. Neither map nor resource icons should rotate.
Walking beneath an emblem must not affect collision or pressure-plate activation.
Regular and shortcut coin pickups should be round golden 3D models with glowing
rims and a rift mark on both faces. Their server anchors stay still; collection
hides the model/light for the collector without changing another player's coin.
Sunset Ruins must not display the CRACKING STONES sign.
Keep standing through the next queue: the selected plate should count again.
Walk onto parkour entry: the clock starts once. Holding still must not reset it.
Hear the calm lobby track, each intense map score, and a smooth return after
escape or practice exit. Check mute, two simultaneous player roles and audio
loading errors. Confirm drifting auroras, twinkling stars and floating shards
stay decorative and leave the courses visible on phone and desktop.

13. QUIETER COURSE FEEDBACK
Clear courses 1-4: no medal card, Next Course notice or repeated pickup text
should cover the next route. Course dots, coins, stamina and dash meters still
update. Earn Rift Scout during a run: the title equips without a popup.
Practice and final race finishes show a small medal/time badge for 3/2.5 seconds.
Check phone portrait, landscape and desktop: badge clears movement, practice and
replay controls. Real action failures and round results still show compact notices.
Open Mastery and Shop afterward: records, medals and earned styles must remain.

14. LAUNCH FEATURES AND HUMAN MULTIPLAYER
Use at least two human players on separate devices after private publishing.
Studio client simulations do not constitute this human playtest.
One player completes Learn to Run while others race. Verify jump, real sprint
travel and an approved dash are required, and Skip returns safely to the lobby.
Check six side buttons and the optional invitation on phone portrait/landscape.
Record a legitimate 24-step parkour best, leave/rejoin, and replay it as a ghost.
Only its owner should see it; the ghost must never collide or affect timing.
A slower attempt must preserve the best. Check the free Ascent Ace title unlock.
Complete a daily goal, verify one fixed reward, then rejoin and repeat the
action: no second reward. Observe a UTC day rollover, preserving other progress.
Inspect catcher floor outlines during a real chase, a dodge and a canceled grab.
Two VIP owners should spar in the arena while a non-owner races. Confirm 25
damage per approved shot, cover blocking and no gear effects outside the arena.
Play all three maps with at least two runners. Verify split course progression,
finisher lobby access, late joiners, captures, respawns and disconnect cleanup.
Record actual human counts, device, map, confusing captures and pass/fail in the
observation sheet. Do not mark human testing passed from automated clients.
After publishing, check Analytics dashboard delivery. Studio sends no events.
