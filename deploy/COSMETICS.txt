HOW THEY WORK
The server checks balance, goals, role/phase and pass entitlement before equipping.
Coin purchases persist; equipping an owned item never charges twice.
One aura and one title use independent slots and persist through rejoin.
The existing profile store name stays unchanged and older profiles migrate with
empty new cosmetic slots, preserving their coins, stats and trails.
Goal eligibility derives from saved progress. Paid ownership never comes from a
saved flag or a client claim. A saved VIP selection requires verified ownership
before it appears again.

Native modeled auras contain 6-16 massless, noncolliding, nonqueryable parts.
They follow the avatar with welds and require no uploaded image or animation IDs.
Catchers keep their cosmetics with extra clearance around the creature body.
Capture removes the adornments for the cinematic, then catcher conversion restores
eligible selections. Role changes and respawns rebuild the selected appearance.
Cosmetics add no speed, stamina, dash power, hitbox size or catching range.
